How It Works

1

Flag it

Spot an AI-generated video? Flag it in one click — from the video page, the popup, or a right-click.

2

Community consensus

Flags from independent users are weighted by reputation. Enough agreement crosses the threshold.

3

Hidden for everyone

The video disappears from feeds for every ByeAI user — with a placeholder so you stay in control.
